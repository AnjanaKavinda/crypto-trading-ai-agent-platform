## Input and authority boundary

`run_spot_execution_scenario` consumes an exact #91 `LoadedValidationDataset`,
an immutable C-021v2, explicit experimental order intents, policy and C-027v2 cost
assumptions with a trusted pure local cost adapter. Raw market rows, mutable
latest-version selectors and providers are absent from the interface. The kernel
checks dataset/manifest hashes, source membership/digests, selected revision pins,
universe scope and knowledge time, bar finality, units, coverage and continuity.

Only a single historical Spot instrument, DEV/TEST environment and strategy
explicitly permitting BACKTEST are supported. Research-only strategy versions
are rejected; they are not changed or granted additional modes by this work.
Orders are explicit scenario configuration, not C-023 signals, qualifications,
approvals or executable exchange orders. Strategy text descriptions are never
converted into price/quantity rules. Quantity, cash and inventory are supplied
simulation parameters, not authoritative sizing or portfolio-risk calculations.

The output is an immutable internal trace with deterministic fill IDs and a
canonical content digest. It is not a C-027v3 canonical backtest result. The
existing publication guard remains intact; a completed scenario does not imply
strategy profitability, data-quality certification, statistical support or
readiness to trade.

## Versioned v1 execution assumptions

Method: `spot-bar-execution-scenario-v1`. Configuration is part of trace/fill
identity. Arithmetic uses a local 120-digit Decimal context with explicit
ROUND_HALF_EVEN; lot allocation rounds down. No stochastic behavior exists in v1;
the explicit seed must be zero. No wall clock or random ID is generated by the
kernel.

- An intent may act only when `decision_time + latency < bar_open`. Equality is
  intentionally deferred. Every declared known revision must be selected,
  finalized and available by the decision time; the historical universe must
  also be available and effective then.
- Completed-bar volume multiplied by an explicit participation rate is a shared
  capacity budget, rounded down to the configured quantity step. V1 is a declared
  bar-volume approximation, not proof of actual intrabar liquidity. Zero volume
  yields no fill. No order gets an independent copy of the same capacity.
- Allocation is deterministic: protective OCO stop legs first, then decision
  time and order ID. Fills are observed at the revision's availability time.
  No balance learned after a bar's open may fund its orders; cash and inventory
  acquired within a bar cannot fund another order in that same bar. This may
  conservatively skip bars with late account knowledge.
- Market orders use the eligible bar's open as an explicit price approximation.
  Limits require a touch and use the adverse limit/range boundary instead of
  choosing a favorable open. Long protective stop-market orders use the worse
  of stop/open price when touched, including a downward gap. Buy stops and short
  positions are unsupported.
- A stop and target in the same OCO group must be matching SELL legs with the
  same quantity, decision evidence and validity. Stop below target is required.
  If both are touched, stop-first handling is explicit. The first partial/full
  fill cancels the peer; the unfilled part of the chosen leg can remain active.
- Expiry/cancellation before an eligible open prevents filling. A terminal time
  inside or at the end of an eligible bar is INCONCLUSIVE because OHLC cannot
  establish whether a touch preceded it. End-of-data cancels/expires remaining
  quantities without inventing a closing fill or realizing an open inventory.
- SELL allocation is capped by inventory known at the open. BUY allocation must
  be affordable, including explicit costs, from cash known then. Insufficient
  cash is INCONCLUSIVE; no margin, borrowing or automatic sizing is inferred.

## Cost seam

Fee, spread, slippage, latency and explicit NOT_APPLICABLE Spot funding must be
required C-027v2 categories. Extra required categories, unknown applicability,
scope/digest mismatch or mismatched latency are unsupported. The request includes
the exact policy, order, quantity, gross price, decision/observation times,
allocation-bar interval, source-revision identity/digest and cost set.

A quote binds the full request digest, assumption-set ID/digest and exact adapter
reference. Monetary components must be finite/nonnegative and cannot silently
be zero when their pinned assumption is positive. Two calls with the identical
request must agree; the production adapter must also be versioned, pure and
repeatable across runs. Known unavailable/stale models raise
`SimulationUnavailable`; no free-cost fallback exists. Unexpected programming
errors propagate instead of being hidden as successful simulations.

Issue #93 supplies the bounded configured Spot TAKER profile and production
`SpotExecutionCostAdapter`. The internal request binds the allocation bar's
actual start/end interval and exact source-revision ID/digest in addition to the
order, fill quantity/gross price, observed time, policy, and C-027v2 set. Profile
effective validity is checked against that bar interval, not a guessed interval
from a later availability timestamp. Configured costs are not represented as a
verified exchange fee feed. See
[`issue-93-spot-cost-models.md`](issue-93-spot-cost-models.md) for formulas,
identity, and failure behavior. Other test-only quotes remain synthetic and are
not substitutes for the production adapter.

## Verification and remaining gates

Golden synthetic scenarios cover next-bar timing, exact cash/inventory and
partial-fill amounts, capacity sharing, no same-bar reuse, limit non-fills,
stop/target ambiguity, gap stops, expiry/cancellation, missing/future evidence,
units/coverage/source tampering, unsupported policy and invalid or nonrepeatable
cost quotes. Replay remains identical under a different ambient Decimal precision
and reordered input orders. Future bars cannot change earlier fill economics.

The PostgreSQL integration persists a synthetic historical closure, loads it
through #91 in read-only transactions across fresh sessions, and reproduces the
same execution trace. No retained database, real provider or exchange is used.
